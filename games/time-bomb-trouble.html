Time Bomb Trouble

A game I made for a jam back in the beginning of 2024. The jam's restriction was that the game needed to be 20 seconds long, so I wanted to create a game which could be beaten in 20 seconds if you knew what you were doing, but which would take much longer to figure that out.
I think the game isn't too bad, although the music leaves much to be desired.
I remember being excited that the music could change in a predictable way according to how far along the 20 seconds the player was, but I didn't use this to its full potential. At some point I might revisit this idea and make music that synchronises with optimal gameplay.

You can play it on itch.io or here.
